Ballpark — User management

How users sign in, what roles exist, and who can do what.

Sign in

  • Google — one-click sign-in
  • Magic Link — email a one-time link (no password)
  • No passwords stored; auth handled by Supabase
  • Optional MFA available later (config only, no code change)

Account lifecycle

  • New user signs in → onboarding (role + company name) → admin of their new org
  • Invited user receives email → signs in → joined to inviter's org with assigned role
  • Existing user signs in → lands in their org's Home

Roles Two flags, not five enums

A user's effective role is derived from two things: which type of org they're in (agency / supplier / ballpark), and whether they're flagged admin on that membership. One toggle per user per org.

Effective role Who they are · What they do
Agency · Admin org_type=agency, is_admin=true Owner / manager of an agency. Invites team. Manages billing. Creates projects, runs marketplace, signs SOWs.
Agency · Member org_type=agency, is_admin=false An event planner on the team. Builds carts, messages suppliers, replies in inbox. No billing, no inviting.
Supplier · Admin org_type=supplier, is_admin=true Owner of a supplier business. Invites team. Manages catalogue + payouts. Responds to briefs.
Supplier · Member org_type=supplier, is_admin=false Someone on the supplier's team. Replies to briefs, adjusts items. No payout settings.
Ballpark Admin org_type=ballpark Anthropic-internal. Cross-org tooling for support, config, debug. Read-mostly across all orgs.

Multi-org support Built in, hidden in v1

A single login can belong to multiple organisations. Useful when:

  • A consultant works across two agencies
  • A supplier owner also runs a small agency
  • Internal Ballpark team needs to assist any org

Schema supports many-to-many from day one. v1 UX shows just the user's primary org — no org switcher yet. Adding the switcher later is UI-only.

Sign-in flow

1. Login Google / Magic Link
2. Identify Existing user? Yes → Home. No → Onboarding.
3. Land In their org's Home, role applied.
Privacy: Ballpark uses Supabase Auth. No passwords are stored by the app. Email + name come from Google or the user's Magic Link sign-in. Users can delete their account from Settings at any time.
