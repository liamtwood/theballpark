# Ballpark — Final Category Taxonomy v2

The definitive two-level category → subcategory structure, plus AI
classification guide. 15 categories. 131 subcategories. Tags are a
separate system (faceted attributes) — the per-category tag dimensions
live in TAXONOMY_REVIEW.md Step 3.

## Decisions applied

1. Equipment rides inside its discipline category (no separate Equipment category)
2. Construction merged into Stand Structure
3. Rigging & Power → AV
4. Comedian & Speaker → keep merged
5. Live Streaming → AV owns the stream
6. Displays & Stands → Graphics (portable displays)
7. Site Survey / Recce → Other
8. Scent / Aroma → Event Accessories only (not Florals)
9. Insurance → Health & Safety only (not Logistics)
10. FOH Manager → tag (seniority), not subcategory

## AI Classification Guide

### Rules for the AI classifier

```
RULE 1 — CATEGORY = what it IS, not where it's used.
RULE 2 — SUBCATEGORY = the TYPE of product/service.
RULE 3 — Equipment rides inside its discipline.
RULE 4 — Stand Structure = the finished thing (construction trades are subcategories of it).
RULE 5 — If in doubt, ask: "What supplier sells this?"
RULE 6 — One category, one subcategory per item.
RULE 7 — Tags handle the attributes (how, where, style, cuisine, material).
```

### Category definitions

```
STAND STRUCTURE — Physical structure/build/installation, incl. construction
  trades (joinery, metalwork, paint, install/de-rig) and flooring.
LIGHTING — All lighting fixtures and design. NOT electrical infrastructure.
AV & TECHNOLOGY — Audio, video, screens, projection, streaming, interactive
  tech, rigging, power distribution, connectivity.
FURNITURE & FIXTURES — Hired furniture, display units, seating, tables.
CATERING — Food and drink services. Staff → Staffing. Equipment stays here.
FLORALS — Event floristry and botanical installations. NOT scent/aroma.
GRAPHICS & SIGNAGE — Printed, vinyl, branded materials. Incl. portable
  display stands. NOT built exhibition stands.
STAFFING — All people hired for events, incl. catering staff.
HEALTH & SAFETY — Risk, compliance, insurance, safety services. All insurance.
LOGISTICS & TRANSPORT — Moving things, storing things, site utilities.
ENTERTAINMENT — Live performance, hosted experiences, talent.
PHOTOGRAPHY — Capture and content creation. NOT streaming infra (AV).
EVENT ACCESSORIES — Finishing touches: red carpet, gift bags, lanyards,
  linen, scent design, glassware/crockery hire, pyro.
VENUE — Venue sourcing and hire.
OTHER — Agency line items: PM fee, design fee, contingency, travel, site survey.
```
